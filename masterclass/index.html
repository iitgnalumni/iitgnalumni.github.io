The format

What happens at a MasterClass

Each session is held on campus and ends by honouring the alum who made it possible. Some sessions also include a workshop, a demonstration or a panel, depending on the speaker’s field.

  1. 01

    Welcome and introduction

    The speaker is introduced to students and faculty.

  2. 02

    Alumni keynote

    The alum shares their journey and expertise.

  3. 03

    Discussion and Q&A

    Students ask questions and discuss openly.

  4. 04

    Networking

    Time with students and faculty after the talk.

  5. 05

    Felicitation

    The Institute honours the speaker for their contribution.

For alumni

Share your journey with the students who walk the paths you once did

How to lead a MasterClass

  1. 1Write to us with a few lines about your work and the topic you would like to share.
  2. 2Plan it together. We agree on a date and the format that suits your field: a talk, a workshop, a demonstration or a panel.
  3. 3Meet the students. Share your journey, take their questions and spend time with students and faculty.
  4. 4Join the honour roll of alumni who have given back to IITGN through the series.

Who we invite

Alumni whose journeys can inspire students and offer practical insight, especially those who have:

  • Built successful start-ups or enterprises
  • Contributed significantly to academia or research
  • Held leadership roles in industry, government or public service
  • Created measurable social impact
  • Earned national or international recognition
  • A genuine willingness to inspire and mentor students

About the series

A flagship way for alumni to give back

The Alumni MasterClass Series brings accomplished IITGN alumni back to campus to share their professional journeys with students, bridging classroom learning and the realities of academia, industry, entrepreneurship, public service and research.

Beyond the expertise they share, every session celebrates what our alumni have achieved and strengthens the lifelong bond between them and the Institute.

FAQs

What is the Alumni MasterClass Series?

It is a flagship initiative of the Office of Alumni Relations that brings IITGN alumni back to share their professional journeys and expertise with current students. Each session includes a talk by the alum, an open discussion with students, and time to connect afterwards.

I am an alum. How can I lead a session?

Write to alumnirelations@iitgn.ac.in with a few lines about your work and the topic you would like to share. The office will get in touch to plan a date and a format that suits you.

What can I speak about?

Anything from your journey that can help students: your career path, your research, building a start-up, working in industry or government, or creating social impact. Depending on your field, a session can also be a workshop, a demonstration or a panel discussion.

Can I nominate a batchmate or another alum?

Yes. Send their name, batch and a short note on why students would benefit from hearing them to alumnirelations@iitgn.ac.in, and the office will reach out to them.

Who can attend a MasterClass?

Sessions are held for IITGN students, and faculty members often join for the discussion and networking. Announcements are shared with the campus community ahead of each session.

Are sessions held online, and are they recorded?

The series is held on campus, though several earlier sessions took place online. Not every session is recorded. Where a recording exists, you can watch it from that session’s details on this page; sessions marked “Recorded” in the honour roll have one.
