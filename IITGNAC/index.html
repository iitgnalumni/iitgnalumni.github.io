About IITGN-AC

A home for the IITGN alumni community

The IIT Gandhinagar Alumni Collective (IITGN-AC) is a dedicated platform to foster a strong network among IITGN alumni. It aims to enhance the sense of community, provide opportunities for professional and personal growth, and contribute to the development of IIT Gandhinagar.

As members of IITGN-AC, alumni can engage in various activities, events, and initiatives that promote collaboration and mutual support.

FAQ

Common questions

Who is a member of IITGN-AC?

Every IIT Gandhinagar alumnus is a member by default, as a General Alumni Member.

What is the difference between a General and a Participating Member?

General Alumni Membership is automatic and free. Participating Membership is an upgrade that lets alumni avail of the Collective's benefits, for a one-time lifetime fee of ₹3,000.

I pledged ₹3,000 when I graduated. Do I need to pay again?

No. Alumni who opted for Alumni Association membership by pledging ₹3,000 at graduation already qualify as Participating Members. You can check your name in the list of Participating Members.

Where can I read the rules of the Collective?

The IITGN-AC Constitution sets out how the Collective and its Executive Committee work.
