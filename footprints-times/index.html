Rules this page enforces
  • Headline on a single line.
  • Photo is always a square, whatever size you upload: 664 × 664 by default, and adjustable down to 460 × 460 with "Photo size" (it shrinks from the right and the story column widens to fill the space). A photo smaller than the square gives a blurriness warning.
  • The story is justified on both sides.
  • Name in at most two lines; batch and caption on one line each.
  • The story column starts level with the top of the photo and ends level with its bottom. If the write-up is too long or too short, the page tells you roughly how many words to cut or add.
  • Download stays locked until every red item is fixed.
