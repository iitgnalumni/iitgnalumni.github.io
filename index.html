Who we are

Your lifelong link to IIT Gandhinagar

Graduation should not be the end of the relationship.

The Office of Alumni Relations is the bridge between IIT Gandhinagar and its graduates. We help alumni stay in touch with the Institute and with each other, and we open doors for them to shape the IITGN of tomorrow.

Our aim is not to ask alumni for more. It is to give them more ways to belong. Every alumnus can contribute in the way that suits them, through time, expertise, networks or resources, and as mentors, advisors, collaborators, recruiters or donors.

IITGN alumni are building careers in industry, research, academia, entrepreneurship, government and the social sector. With 73% of alumni having contributed to IITGN at least once, the Institute has one of the strongest cultures of alumni giving in India. Whatever you need from the Institute after graduation, start with us.

About IIT Gandhinagar

A young IIT with a different idea of education

Established in 2008, IIT Gandhinagar sits on a 400-acre green campus on the banks of the Sabarmati. It is part of the IIT system and is known for its emphasis on research, interdisciplinary learning and global engagement. Around 3,300 students study here today, taught by more than 150 faculty members.

Learning goes well beyond the classroom. Every new undergraduate begins with an immersive Foundation Programme, and students take courses in design, the humanities and the sciences alongside their discipline. Programmes span engineering (including artificial intelligence, integrated circuit design and maritime engineering), the natural sciences, cognitive science, and the humanities and social sciences.

Research is organised around today’s big challenges, through interdisciplinary centres for cognitive and brain sciences, sustainable development, biomedical engineering, AI-driven innovations, archaeological sciences, safety engineering, design and innovation, creative learning, and research commercialisation. The Institute sits among some of India’s leading institutions, including ISRO’s Space Applications Centre, IIM Ahmedabad, NID, PRL and GIFT City, and works with partners around the world, including through the AIT–IITGN Collaborative Center in Thailand.

The campus also gives back to its neighbours. Through Nyasa, the Institute supports the education of children of migrant construction workers around the campus.
