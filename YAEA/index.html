The Young Alumni Excellence Award, IIT Gandhinagar’s honour for its young alumni, is presented this year to three alumni whose early careers have already made a remarkable impact in their fields and in society.

About the Award

The Young Alumni Excellence Award (YAEA) is an initiative to honour and recognise the achievements of IIT Gandhinagar alumni who have made outstanding contributions to their respective fields.

IIT Gandhinagar has a highly engaged alumni community, and this award aims to encourage and recognise the early achievements of these alumni and inspire them to take up more challenging and impactful roles in their careers.

By recognising and honouring young alumni who have excelled in various fields, we hope to inspire IITGN students and future graduates to pursue their passions and make a difference in the world.

Award categories

    Eligibility

    IITGN alumni who have completed five years since obtaining their first degree from IITGN and are under 45 years of age.

    Selection

    Awardees are selected by the Chairman of the Board of Governors on the recommendation of a committee constituted by the Director of IITGN:

    1. Director, IIT Gandhinagar (Chairman)
    2. Professor-in-Charge / Dean, Alumni Relations
    3. Dean, Institutional Advancement
    4. One current or former Director of any IIT
    5. One present or former Council Nominee to the IITGN Board of Governors
